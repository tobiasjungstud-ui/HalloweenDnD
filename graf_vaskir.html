Rules

Quiet, slow, polite — he never threatens. Under 6 HP he stops: “Enough. Let us talk.” → Gruppe entscheidet: Angebot oder weiterkämpfen.

0 HP: he turns to dust. All hunters at 0 HP: ending “Lost”.

What he says

What do you want? — “Company. Somebody to talk to. Four hundred years is a long time alone.”

Let her go! — “And what do you give me? Nothing? That is not a deal.”

What happens to the village without you? — “Hunger. And in February the wolves come back.”

Will you hurt her? — “Hurt? No. They stay. They are never hungry, never cold — and never old.”

What about Anneke? — “She is still here. She sleeps in the chapel. She is never cold — and never old.”
