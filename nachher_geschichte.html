1 · Plan your story 5 minutes

Where?a place: a castle, a forest, a ship, a school at night …
Who?two heroes — their names and what they can do
What is scary?a creature or a secret
What does it want?a deal, a promise, help, revenge …

2 · Write your story 15 minutes · 120–180 words · on the laptop

Like our game: your heroes must make a decision. Write the story up to the decision, then two short endings.

  • a title
  • the story is in the past (walked, saw, was running)
  • one past perfect (had seen, had gone)
  • one person speaks: “…”
  • a decision: A or B?
  • Ending A — and Ending B
  • three words from the game (below)

Words from the game: a count · a deal · to promise · instead of · an offer · to escape · a servant · to scream · fog · a courtyard · disappointed · dust

3 · Check your story with AI copy, paste your story at the end, send

Use the AI prompt from your teacher. Paste your story at the end and send it.

You are a friendly English teacher. We are two students, 14 years old, English level B1. Below is our Halloween story.

Do NOT rewrite our story and do NOT give us a corrected version. We want to correct it ourselves.

1. SPELLING: Find the 5 most important spelling mistakes. Make a table with 3 columns: the wrong word in its sentence | the correct spelling | a short tip to remember it.

2. GRAMMAR – PAST FORMS: Our story should be told in the past. Check ONLY the past forms:
- past simple (regular verbs with -ed, irregular verbs like go → went, see → saw),
- past progressive (was/were + -ing),
- past perfect (had + past participle) for something that happened BEFORE another thing in the past.
Find the 2–3 most important problems with these past forms. For each problem:
- explain the rule in 1–2 easy sentences, with a short German explanation in brackets,
- show ONE example from our text: what we wrote → how it should be,
- then quote the other places in our text with the same mistake, but do NOT correct them. We will do that.
If we did not use the past perfect at all, show us one place in our story where it would fit — but let us write the sentence ourselves.

3. At the end, tell us one thing we did really well.

Use simple English and keep your answer short.

OUR STORY:
[paste your story here]

Then correct your story yourself: first the 5 spelling mistakes, then the grammar places the AI found.

4 · Play it! 10 minutes

  1. Find another pair.
  2. You are the DM: read your story to them — stop at the decision and ask: “What do you do?”
  3. They choose A or B. Read that ending.
  4. Then switch: now they are the DM.

4 · Gallery walk 10 minutes

  1. Put your story on the wall.
  2. Read three other stories.
  3. On each one, leave a sticky note: one thing you liked + one question (“Why did … ?”).
  4. At the end, vote: the scariest monster and the best decision.

4 · Story doctor 15 minutes

  1. Swap stories with another pair.
  2. Check their story with the list in part 2. Tick what is there.
  3. Underline two mistakes and write one tip under the story.
  4. Get your story back and make it better.
